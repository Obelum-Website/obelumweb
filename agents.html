Questions

Before you join.

Do I need a RERA broker card?

Yes, to close deals under Obelum. Without one you can still join as a Partner and refer clients.

What split do I get?

90% of the commission on every deal from day one.

When am I paid?

7 days after the deal is registered.

Is there a monthly fee?

To confirm.

Do my clients stay mine?

Yes. Your clients and your relationships remain yours.

Whose licence do I work under?

Obelum Properties, RERA ORN 65292, licence 1656005.

How does Obelum earn?

From its share of each deal and from partner services, never from your clients directly.

About this service

Know a broker who would do better keeping 90%? Introduce them to Obelum. Once they join and start closing, you earn 10% of OBELUM’s margin on every deal they close, with no cap on how many brokers you refer or how much you earn.

You don’t need to close anything yourself. We onboard your broker, support their deals, and pay your share as each deal completes.

What’s included

  • 10% of OBELUM’s margin on each deal your broker closes
  • Paid on every deal for two years, with no cap
  • Refer as many brokers as you like
  • A clear record of your referrals and their deals
  • Your share paid 7 days after each deal is collected
  • Your broker keeps 90% and their own clients

Common questions

Who can I refer?

Any broker with a valid RERA card, or one who is getting theirs, who isn’t already working with Obelum.

How much do I earn?

10% of OBELUM’s margin on every deal your broker closes, with no cap.

For how long?

On every deal they close for two years from the day they join.

When am I paid?

7 days after Obelum collects the commission on each deal.

Do I need to be an Obelum agent?

No. Anyone can refer a broker to Obelum.
