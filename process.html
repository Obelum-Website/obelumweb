How your money moves

The developer doesn’t get paid until it builds

When you buy off-plan, you don’t pay the developer directly. Your money goes into the project’s escrow account and is released in stages as construction progresses.

  1. Step 1

    You pay

    You transfer each instalment to the escrow account named in your contract, never to a personal account.

  2. Transfer in the project’s name
  3. Step 2

    Project escrow account

    An account at a DLD-approved bank, exclusive to this project. The developer can’t use the money for anything else.

    Supervised by DLD and RERA
  4. Only with certified construction progress
  5. Step 3

    The developer gets paid

    The bank releases the money in stages, based on construction progress certified by an engineer and reviewed by the DLD.

Money is released by construction stageIllustrative example
  1. Foundations
  2. Structure
  3. Façade
  4. Services
  5. Finishes

If construction doesn’t progress, the money doesn’t leave the escrow account. If RERA cancels the project, buyers are refunded from that same account.

Who’s who

The players that protect you

Six concepts worth understanding before you buy. We explain them without the jargon.

01

Dubai Land Department (DLD)

The government body that registers every property, sale and lease in Dubai. Think of it as the land registry and the cadastre in one.

02

RERA

The DLD’s regulatory agency. It licenses and supervises developers, projects, agencies and agents, approves listings and monitors escrow accounts.

03

Escrow account

The dedicated bank account for each off-plan project (Law 8/2007). Your money stays there and is only released to the developer as construction progresses.

04

Oqood

The provisional registration of your off-plan purchase with the DLD. It proves the unit is yours while it is being built, and becomes the title deed at handover.

05

SPA (Sale and Purchase Agreement)

The contract with the developer. It sets the price, the payment plan, the expected handover date and what happens if either side defaults.

06

Title deed

The final ownership deed the DLD issues in your name once the property is handed over and fully paid.
